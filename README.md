# promtathon-2025

The DSU X TEMPETE AIML Club recently hosted The Promptathon, an exciting AI competition that brought together creative minds to test their problem-solving and prompt engineering skills using tools like ChatGPT, Bard, Claude, and more. Teams of 2–4 members had the opportunity to design and refine prompts for real-world AI challenges, enhance their understanding of effective prompt crafting, and collaborate with fellow innovators. The event was a fantastic hands-on experience that showcased talent, teamwork, and innovation in AI.
